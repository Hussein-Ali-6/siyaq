Field notes

A family bakery has refused a generous offer from a national chain that wanted to buy it. Its owner, Ruth Marlow, said no because the chain planned to use cheaper ingredients, and she would rather keep the bakery small than lose the trust it has built over four decades. Customers and other shop owners on the street welcomed the decision.

The meeting ran for two hours, and every item on the agenda was discussed except one. The cost of the project was the elephant in the room. It had doubled since the spring, and nobody at the table wanted to be the first to say so.

The auditors expected a mess and found the opposite. Her records were meticulous down to the last receipt. Every payment had a date, a reason and a signature beside it.
