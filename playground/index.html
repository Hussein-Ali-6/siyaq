The board turned the offer down

The board met on Monday. After a short debate it turned the offer down. Nobody was surprised, least of all the founders, who had spent the summer burning through their funding and spilling the beans to half the office.

The deal fell through at the last minute, and the investors walked away with nothing. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the licensee shall indemnify the licensor against any claims by others arising from its use of the software.
